Who We Are

We started EcoGuard Industries in 2026, after a hackathon competition by MICTSETA & AlgoAtWork, hosted at Ehlazeni TVET College, as a group of five, we had to do research on specfic problem based on the domain given to us, and build the solution protoype. Right after the competition, instead of disbanding and going our own individual ways, we came to an agreement to further and enhance our prototype, and as so we established EcoGuard Industries.

Our Mission

Our misson, is to make technology that protects, serves and promotes coexistence between and amongst the ecology, wildlife and humans.

EcoWildGuard

EcoWildGuard is the first system, we built during the hackathon, it is on the basic level, a wildlife, nature reserve road crossing detection system.

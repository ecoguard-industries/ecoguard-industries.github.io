01 // Who we are

Five people. One prototype. Still building.

EcoGuard Industries began at the MICT SETA and AlgoAtWork hackathon, hosted at Ehlanzeni TVET College. The five of us worked as a team to research a problem in our assigned domain and build a working prototype.

When the competition ended, we chose not to go our separate ways. We agreed to keep developing the prototype together, and in 2026 that decision became EcoGuard Industries.

We are an early-stage South African company. We are testing, learning and working towards real-world pilots.

03 // Flagship system

Prototype // Active development

EcoWildGuard

Intelligent protection for wildlife-sensitive transport corridors.

EcoWildGuard is a developing prototype that combines physical hardware and software. It started as our hackathon entry and is now our main focus. It has not been deployed commercially.

About the sensors: the current prototype uses simpler sensors for proof-of-concept testing. They detect presence or movement and do not identify species. Future versions may use radar, thermal sensing, computer vision or other detection technology better suited to field conditions.

  • Roadside detection
  • Local automated response
  • Connected telemetry
  • Operator dashboard
  • Incident monitoring
  • Spatial / 3D visualisation
  • Future advanced sensing

05 // Mission

Our mission

Our mission is to develop technology that protects, serves and promotes coexistence between people, wildlife and the environments we share.

  • Environmental responsibility

    We consider the impact of what we build, from materials to power use.

  • Practical engineering

    We favour designs that work in the field over designs that only look good in a demo.

  • Repairability

    Hardware should be understandable, serviceable and fixable by the people who use it.

  • Efficient systems

    Do the work at the edge where it makes sense, and keep power and data use modest.

  • Local innovation

    We are building in South Africa, for the conditions we know and the problems close to us.

  • Long-term sustainability

    We want systems that can be maintained and improved over years, not months.
